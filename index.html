Privacy Policy – Snowfall Christmas Watch Face

Effective: November 2026 · Developer: Danny McReynolds

Snowfall Christmas is a watch face. It does not collect, store, share, or transmit any personal data. It has no internet access, no analytics, no ads, and no accounts.

Complication data (such as battery, steps, sunrise/sunset, or weather) is supplied by your watch's own complication providers and displayed on-device only; the watch face never stores or sends it anywhere.

Children: the app does not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children.

Changes: any updates to this policy will be posted at this URL.
